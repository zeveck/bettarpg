# Brief: Betta Fish RPG (remake)

**In one sentence:** a little betta swims out of Paddy Village across the terraced rice
paddies to find out why fish are vanishing, and can settle it with fins or with friendship.

**The feeling:** cozy and a bit brave. A bright, sun-dappled paddy you want to explore, with
a shiver of mystery in the darker water, and a warm, silly heart (party hats, Happy Balloon
Time). Small fish, big adventure.

## What they asked for

- [ ] Remake *their* Betta Fish RPG, keeping their assets and ideas (`--remake`)
- [ ] With lots of freedom: build what it was reaching for (`--leeway lots`)
- [ ] A reviewed plan only, no build yet (`--plan-only`)
- [ ] Shared for comments as a page, with $10 of mock pictures (`--share-design --mockups $10`)
- [ ] Agreed: smooth the early difficulty
- [ ] Agreed: cheat keys only in a debug mode

## What the original clearly aimed at (from its code, notes and dialogue)

- [ ] Play as a young betta, choose name and color; color kept through armor upgrades
- [ ] Paddy Village hub with Elder Finn, Shopkeeper Coral, Guard Captain Reef, Bubble the Brave, Innkeeper Seaweed
- [ ] The mystery: fish disappearing from the outer paddies; "dark shadows when the rice stalks sway"
- [ ] Bubble wants to come with you
- [ ] "The big city on the next terrace"
- [ ] Danger grows with distance from the village; deadly dark edge zones
- [ ] Turn-based battles: Attack, Bubble Blast, Gravel Grenade, Happy Balloon Time, Swim Away
- [ ] Befriending as a real alternative path → "Peace in the Paddies"
- [ ] Armor at levels 3/5/7; Betta Bites; Fish Mart; the Inn
- [ ] The Dunkleosteus submarine for 100 Betta Bites
- [ ] The Prehistoric / Gargantuan Gar boss
- [ ] NPCs who react to what you've done (their dialogue proposal)
- [ ] Rice-paddy authenticity: bettas live in paddies, not oceans
- [ ] Full keyboard play

## Who and where

Casual players and RPG fans, any age. Desktop and phones (landscape). Sessions of 10–20
minutes; the whole game in about 30–45 minutes, saved between sessions.

## Style

The original's own art is the reference: chunky, dark-outlined pixel art with bold flat
color and simple shading (player_betta.png is the anchor). Borrow its palette and line;
keep the world in the same pixel scale. Top-down camera like a classic handheld RPG
overworld, but original in every detail.

## Settings and decisions

Platform both, web, medium scope, $20 budget ($10 for mockups), 2 review rounds, 2D.
